Weekly radar: AI, sanctions, and currencies

Three brief signals for reading how technology, finance, and state capacity are coupling together.

By Javier Salazar Segales April 4, 2026
China and Asia Weekly radar: AI, sanctions, and currencies Artificial intelligence and global power / International economics

In the short term, global transformations filter through apparently technical measures. Over the long term, those measures redefine hierarchies.

Key signals

AI enters sectoral regulation The debate is no longer abstract. Governments are beginning to demand traceability, risk assessment, and concrete governance by sector.
Sanctions generate redundancy systems Payments, insurance, suppliers, and logistics networks adapt in order to survive in environments of greater political friction.
Local currencies gain tactical use They do not replace the dominant system, but they do open transactional and financing spaces that are less exposed to geopolitical volatility.

Why it matters

The common lesson is clear: technology and finance already operate as part of the same strategic board.
